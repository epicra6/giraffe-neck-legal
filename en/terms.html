GIRAFFE NECK Terms of Use

Effective date: September 27, 2026

1. About this service

GIRAFFE NECK is a photography experience in which people interpret a shared theme in their own way. The service is for people aged 16 or older. Participation is subject to availability and account eligibility. Sign in with Apple and meet the participation requirements before joining.

2. Events and submissions

Each round lasts the same seven minutes worldwide, measured by the server. Each person may submit one photo per round. Joining late, an incorrect device clock or a delayed notification does not extend the deadline. You may retake your photo before confirming submission. Whether the theme or event time is revealed in advance depends on the round. Admission may be limited by capacity, service availability or connectivity.

3. Photos and rights

Submit photos you took and have the right to use. Obtain permission when photographing people and respect privacy, likeness and intellectual property rights. You retain your rights to your photos. You allow Epicra to use them as necessary to provide the service, including safety checks, image conversion, delivery to eligible participants and report handling. Promotional use by the operator requires separate permission.

4. Safety checks and publication

Photos undergo safety checks, including AI processing. These checks do not score photographic skill or interpretation of the theme. Checks can be mistaken, delayed or fail. Acceptance of a submission does not guarantee publication. Unchecked photos are not published. A photo may be withheld because of a service failure rather than its content.

5. WORLD completion and viewing

WORLD completes using approved photos eligible for publication, and its count is fixed at completion. A photo that misses completion is not added later, even if subsequently approved. Eligible participants whose photos are delayed by the service can still view the completed WORLD and keep their participation record. Explicit rejection, suspension and expiry have separate access restrictions. WORLD photos are normally viewable for 30 days; the expiry appears in the app. Deletion or hiding can make the visible number differ from the original completed count.

6. Reactions and sharing

You may select one reaction type per photo, switch it or remove it. Only the photo's contributor can see its counts and breakdown. Free share cards include your own published photo, event details and abstract colors extracted from some published photos. Other people's recognizable photos are not exported. Saving and sharing are initiated by you and are subject to the destination's terms. Deletion or expiry in GN does not remove exported images. Screenshots by viewers cannot be completely prevented.

7. Prohibited conduct

Sexual exploitation, sexual or nude content, excessive violence or gore, hate, threats, harassment, disclosure of private information, rights infringement, illegal conduct, dangerous photography, promotion, solicitation, spam, unauthorized access, impersonation, evasion of restrictions and interference with the service are prohibited. See the Community Guidelines.

8. Reports, hiding and restrictions

Use the photo detail menu to report content or hide a contributor. A report is reviewed by the operator and does not guarantee immediate removal. Hiding applies to current and future WORLDs. Contact support if you need help reversing an accidental hide. The operator may withhold photos, restrict participation or suspend accounts when necessary for safety. You may also contact support about an incorrect decision.

9. Interruptions

Maintenance, emergencies, connectivity problems, external API failures or cost protections can interrupt functions, stop submissions or cancel an event. Existing submissions and WORLDs remain subject to their status and access conditions. Notification delivery and publication of every photo cannot be guaranteed.

10. Data and account deletion

See the Privacy Policy for data handling. Request account deletion in Settings and verify with Apple. Authentication, photos, participation records, reactions, notification and hiding records are deleted in stages. Diagnostic and audit logs and exported images are handled separately.

11. Consideration for others and responsibility

GIRAFFE NECK values each participant’s consideration for others and responsibility for their own expression. You are responsible for checking your photos, obtaining permission where needed, respecting others’ rights and deciding what to share outside the app.

Users’ photos and interpretations are their own and do not represent Epicra’s views or endorsement. Epicra provides the service and safety measures, but does not guarantee the accuracy of each post, that every necessary permission has been obtained, or that disagreements with other users or third parties will never arise.

Epicra does not promise to negotiate or mediate on behalf of those involved or resolve every individual disagreement. You are not required to contact the other person directly. Use reporting, hiding or support for problems in the app. We review reports and take necessary action, such as withholding content or restricting access, with priority given to safety and urgency.

Epicra remains responsible as required by applicable law. These terms do not remove consumer rights or exclude liabilities that cannot legally be excluded.

We seek to resolve issues through individual consultation and do not designate an exclusive court jurisdiction.
