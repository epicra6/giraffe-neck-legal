Community Guidelines

Interpret the theme freely. There is no single correct answer and this is not a contest of photographic skill.

GIRAFFE NECK grew from respect for one another and the joy of seeing things differently. Pause before taking a photo and consider the people in it and those who will see it. A welcoming WORLD is something we build together. Take responsibility for what you post and share, and respect other people’s boundaries.

Photograph with care

Obtain permission before photographing people. Do not expose addresses, contact details, private documents or other people's personal information. Respect copyright and likeness rights.

Keep WORLD safe

Do not submit sexual or nude content, exploitation, excessive violence, gore, hate, threats or harassment. Do not endanger yourself or others to take a photo. Promotion, solicitation, spam and rights infringement are not welcome.

When something feels wrong

Use the photo detail menu to report a photo or hide that person's photos. Reports are reviewed and do not automatically erase the photo. Hiding applies to current and future WORLDs and remains on your account. There is no undo control in the app yet; contact support about an accidental hide.

Safety decisions

Checks may be mistaken or delayed. Service delays do not mean you violated the rules. Operators can withhold photos or restrict repeated abuse. Contact support if you have concerns about a decision.

If something feels wrong, use reporting or hiding to create distance rather than seeking someone out or sharing their personal information. Reports and constructive feedback are welcome. Describe what happened clearly and without personal attacks.
