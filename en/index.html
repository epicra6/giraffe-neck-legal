GIRAFFE NECK Support

Seven minutes. A bigger world.

Joining

For people aged 16 or older. Sign in with Apple, complete participation preparation and check the event information in the app. Participation is subject to availability and account eligibility. The seven-minute deadline is shared even when you join late.

A photo is taking time to check

Processing can be busy. Do not delete and resubmit the same photo; check the status shown in the app. If processing misses WORLD completion, your participation record remains. Eligible delayed participants can still enjoy the completed WORLD. Unchecked photos stay private and are not added after completion.

Saving and sharing

Create a card from a completed WORLD containing your published photo. Save to Photos adds it to your photo library and requires add-only permission the first time. Share this card opens sharing options. Exported images are not removed by GN's expiry or deletion.

Reports and hiding

Use the photo detail menu to report content. Reports go to the operator for review and do not necessarily remove a photo immediately. Hide this person's photos applies to current and future WORLDs. There is no undo button in the app. Contact support with the event date if you hid someone accidentally.

Trouble signing in with Apple

GN does not issue an app-specific password. If you forget your Apple Account password, follow Apple’s reset instructions. GN cannot send an Apple password reset email. https://support.apple.com/102656

Account deletion

Open Settings, choose Delete account and verify with Apple. Deletion runs in stages. Contact support if you cannot reauthenticate. We never ask for your Apple password. Deleting your GN account does not delete your Apple Account. Returning after deletion is complete requires participation preparation for a new GN account; previous photos and participation history are not restored. If you cannot return while deletion is still processing, allow time and check again.

Caring for this space together

Posting and external sharing are each user’s decisions. Please respect other people’s rights and feelings. Support does not promise to mediate every disagreement or fulfill every request. We do accept safety reports, bug reports and concerns about rights infringement.

Contact

Our initial-response target is not a guarantee of resolution within a set time. Please keep additional information in the same email conversation to help us review it.
