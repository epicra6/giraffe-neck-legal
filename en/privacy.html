GIRAFFE NECK Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

1. Authentication and participation

We use Sign in with Apple and Firebase Authentication to verify identity. We process an internal user ID, Apple authentication identifiers, confirmation that you are at least 16, the version of participation conditions, invitation and suspension status, and participation records. The app does not request Apple's name or email scopes, create a public profile or ask for your date of birth.

2. Photos and safety checks

We store submitted photos, event references, submission times, admission, safety and publication states and processing results in services including Cloud Storage and Firestore. Display images have embedded metadata, including location, removed during conversion; information visible in the picture remains. The original upload is also stored for processing. Images are sent to Google Cloud Vision and Vertex AI for safety checks. Checks can be mistaken or delayed. Unchecked photos are not published to participants. Operators may review photos for safety or report handling.

3. Viewing and sharing

Approved photos that meet publication conditions are delivered to participants entitled to view that round. A photo that misses WORLD completion is not added later, even after approval. A delayed participant's viewing access does not mean their own photo is published. Free share cards use your own published photo and event details. Surrounding circles use only average colors from some photos you may view; other people's recognizable shapes, faces or text are not exported. Saving to Photos requests add-only permission and does not read your existing albums. Saved or shared cards and screenshots taken by viewers are not removed by deletion in GN or the 30-day viewing limit.

4. Reactions, reports and hiding

We store your selected reaction type, account ID and references to its target to prevent duplicates and show the contributor's breakdown. Other people cannot see those counts. Report reasons, reporter and target references and handling status are used for safety operations. Hidden-contributor references are linked to your account and filter that person's photos from current and future WORLDs.

5. Notifications and usage records

Notification tokens, installation identifiers, language and scheduling/delivery records support event notifications and prevent duplicates. Notifications are optional. Defined milestones in preparation, submission, WORLD and photo viewing are associated with the account and, when relevant, event to provide and improve the experience. These are milestones, not comprehensive tap counts, viewed-photo counts or duration tracking. Before authentication, milestones are kept temporarily in device memory and sent after authentication.

6. Diagnostics and service providers

The app uses Firebase Auth, Functions, App Check, Messaging, Crashlytics, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Run, Cloud Tasks and Scheduler, Google Cloud logging and monitoring, Cloud Vision, Vertex AI, Apple authentication and APNs. Distributed builds use Crashlytics for crashes and some communication errors. The app does not attach photos, its own UID or raw error text to Crashlytics; SDKs process installation, device, OS and diagnostic information. Some server diagnostics include user or device identifiers and processing durations. Authentication and abuse prevention may involve provider processing of IP addresses and app integrity information. We currently use no advertising SDK, advertising identifier, ATT tracking permission, Google Analytics or in-app purchases. SDK diagnostic analytics are separate. Processing may occur outside Japan, depending on the service and configuration. The Epicra board loads public information from GitHub Pages and caches it on your device. GN user IDs, photos and reactions are not sent to that board. The hosting provider may process routine access information such as IP addresses. App Store country and device region filter recommendations on-device. Tapping a recommendation opens Apple's App Store. This is not behavioral advertising and uses no advertising SDK.

7. Retention and deletion

WORLD images become inaccessible at their 30-day expiry and images and reactions are removed by periodic cleanup. Participation metadata remains. Usage milestones expire 30 days after their last update. Reports, hiding and notification records do not all expire with a WORLD. Regular server logs are retained for 30 days and mandatory Google Cloud audit logs for 400 days. Crashlytics and other provider retention periods differ from WORLD expiry and follow provider conditions. Consult the provider documents below for AI processing policies. Account deletion in Settings requires Apple reauthentication. Authentication, user information, photos, participation records, sent and received reactions, notification/delivery references, relevant reports, hiding and usage records are removed in stages. Cleanup may require multiple scheduled passes. Operator audit references are anonymized with a 30-day expiry. Minimal deleted-account records and completed jobs are eligible for cleanup after 24 hours. Log references, provider backups and exported images are not all erased immediately.

8. Support and changes

Settings displays a support ID and app version for you to copy; it does not send them automatically. If you email us, we use your email address, message and voluntarily attached information to respond. Do not send passwords or verification codes. Support information retention: We aim to delete support emails approximately 90 days after the matter is resolved. Information for ongoing matters is retained for as long as needed. Contact epicra6@gmail.com for correction or deletion enquiries. We may need to verify your identity. Changes are announced through this page or the app, with notices and consent where required.
